Glucose → Furfural

Fully QM-validated 10-step reaction chain · r²SCAN-3c / CPCM(water), ORCA 6.1.1, DEFGRID3 throughout · single imaginary mode + exact neutral-SMILES IRC double-end match at every step · agent-discovered C6→C5 retro-aldol bridge

Summary

Ten elementary steps take open-chain D-glucose to furfural. The key feature is an agent-discovered retro-aldol C–C cleavage (step 4) that splits the C6 backbone into formaldehyde + a C5 enediol, which tautomerizes to D-xylose and then follows the classical pentose→furfural dehydration cascade. Every transition state carries exactly one imaginary frequency and an IRC whose two endpoints optimize to the exact neutral reactant and product (a single catalytic water in step 5 is treated as a stripped spectator, per the same acceptance bar as the HMF chain).

10/10 single-imaginary TS 10/10 exact IRC double-end 11/10 barriers DEFGRID3 throughout C6→C5 retro-aldol bridge

Overall C₆H₁₂O₆ → furfural (C₅H₄O₂) + HCHO + 3 H₂O, ΔE = 17.8 kcal/mol. Imaginary modes (cm⁻¹): -434.6 / -2042.5 / -2032.5 / -932.3 / -1630.0 / -766.5 / -1459.8 / -2137.8 / -1541.1 / -1621.5.
